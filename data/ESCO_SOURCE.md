# ESCO occupation dataset provenance

CareerLens AI uses a flattened local subset of the English ESCO v1.1.1 occupation classification. It contains occupation concepts with their descriptions, alternate labels, ESCO codes, and linked essential/optional skills. It contains no job vacancy, employer, location, or salary records.

## Source files

The source CSV files are stored unchanged in `data/esco_source/` and were obtained from the public `tabiya-tech/tabiya-open-dataset` repository, folder `tabiya-esco-v1.1.1/csv/`. That repository documents these files as a CSV transformation based on the original ESCO v1.1.1. The flattened app file `data/esco_occupations.csv` is generated by `scripts/prepare_esco_dataset.py`.

- Occupations: 3,007 records
- Skills: 13,896 records
- Occupation-skill relations: 123,788 records in the downloaded CSV
- Flattened CareerLens occupation profiles: 3,007 records (counts rechecked from the local file)
- ESCO version: v1.1.1, English

Counts above are measured directly from the local CSV files. The flattened output has 3,007 unique occupation profiles and contains 123,788 linked skill labels across those profiles.

## Attribution and reuse

The European Commission ESCO FAQ states that the ESCO classification may be reused free of charge under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU, subject to acknowledgement and a requirement to identify modifications. CareerLens displays the required acknowledgement and identifies its flattened CSV as an adaptation. The upstream Tabiya repository does not include a separate licence file for its CSV transformation, so that transformation's separate licence is marked **Not verified** in the app.

Required ESCO acknowledgement:

> This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission.
